Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India | Former President of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he played a key role in India's civilian space program and military missile development.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and was widely known as the "People's President" because of his connection with students and youth.

Achievements

  • Led India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998
  • Key figure in ISRO and DRDO projects
  • Developed Agni and Prithvi missiles
  • Awarded Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor
  • Authored inspiring books like "Wings of Fire"

Famous Quotes

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

"You have to dream before your dreams can come true."
